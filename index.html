We're so glad you can celebrate with us!

Here's some useful information for the day.

Dress code

There is no strict dress code, normal smart wedding attire is fine.

Transport

The venue does not have good phone reception, so if you'd like to arrange a taxi home it would be best to either pre-book or speak to one of the Bridesmaids and Groomsmen on the day, who will have access to the venue wifi.

Bar

The bar will accept cash or card, so go wild either way!

Children

For those of you bringing young children, there is a quiet space available for naps, speak to someone in the wedding party for access. You'll need to have your own travel cot or pram for them to sleep in and a baby monitor if you'd like one.

We're having a child friendly wedding, so please be comfortable if yours is talking (or even shouting!) during the ceremony. They are more than welcome to leave their seat and run around, be that off to the side, down the aisle, or up next to us.
